Japanese Language Training (N5–N3)

Comprehensive JLPT N5, N4, and N3 training designed specifically for engineers, IT professionals, and university graduates seeking careers in Japan.

Curriculum & Scope

JLPT Course Tracks

JLPT N5 Certification Track

Hiragana, Katakana, basic 100+ Kanji, fundamental sentence patterns, listening audio labs, and conversational greeting drills.

JLPT N4 Intermediate Track

Complex grammar conjunctions, 300+ Kanji, reading comprehension passages, everyday business dialogue, and listening mock tests.

JLPT N3 Career Gateway

Bridging intermediate and fluent technical Japanese, workplace business etiquette, 650+ Kanji, and technical software terminology.

IT Japanese for Engineers

Dedicated vocabulary for software developers, git commands, agile sprint meetings in Japanese, and code review communication.

Japanese Resume (Rirekisho)

Preparation of Japanese format resumes (履歴書) and work history documents (職務経歴書) tailored for Tokyo recruiters.

1-on-1 Interview Coaching

Mock interviews simulating Japanese corporate hiring rounds, behavioral etiquette (Ojigi, Aisatsu), and salary negotiations.
